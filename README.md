## Photos

`Catalogs/images/<model line>/` — `SKU.*` is the main photo, `SKU_2.*` an optional second one. Preference: open and folded (clip side) in one shot → open plus folded clip side as two shots → best available photo of the exact variant.

## Sources

- [Spydiewiki](https://www.spydiewiki.com) model pages (variation tables)
- Spyderco forum thread [Sprints/Exclusives since Jan 2019](https://forum.spyderco.com/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=90980)
- [spyderco.com](https://spyderco.com) product listings (official model numbers and photos)
- Dealer and retailer product pages for photos

Model numbers follow spyderco.com or retailer spelling where the wiki differs; the wiki spelling is kept in the Alt SKU column.

Review checklist for new and existing lists: [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md). Building or updating a family: [tools/README.md](tools/README.md); AI agents follow the [spyderco-catalog skill](.claude/skills/spyderco-catalog/SKILL.md).
